Title Screen

The Title Screen is the first room seen in THE:MUSEUM, appearing immediately upon launching the game. It takes the appearance of an incredibly basic video game title screen, although with strange textural choices, the most apparent being the background image, which is a repeated illustration (ostensibly from the 19th century) of 2 dogs, a woven basket, a wooden plank, and a wooden floor and wall. The Title Screen is also notable for being the first time the player sees CC, the Museumkeeper.

Interactions

  • Hovering over CC causes her to "smile" at you.
  • Pressing the Start button causes Speck (a friendly rival of CC's) to fly by extremely quickly, causing CC to leave also.

Entryways

It only appears when you launch the game. In the scope of the game world, you can't re-enter the room after leaving it.

Exits

The only exit is to press the Start button, where you'll be taken to the Dead Vermin Residence.
