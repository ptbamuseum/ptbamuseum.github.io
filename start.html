Title Screen

The Title Screen is the first room seen in THE:MUSEUM, appearing immediately upon starting the game. It takes the appearance of an incredibly basic video game title screen, although with strange textural choices, the most apparent being the background image, which is a repeated illustration (ostensibly from the 19th century) of 2 dogs, a woven basket, a wooden plank, and a wooden floor and wall. The Title Screen is also notable for being the first time the player sees CC, the Museumkeeper.

Interactions

  • Hovering over CC causes her to "smile" at you.
  • Pressing Start causes Speck (a friendly rival of CC's) to fly by extremely quickly, causing CC to leave also.

Exits

The only exit is to press "start", in which you'll be taken to the Dead Vermin Residence/.
