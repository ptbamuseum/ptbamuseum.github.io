Dead Vermin Residence

The Dead Vermin Residence is the first room that offers an objective to the player, consisting of 4 puzzles the player must solve to progress. The room has a table with a screen on top, as well as a window. Other objects in the room change depending on the image displayed on the screen, which may be changed by clicking on either it or the table. The window may also be interacted with, usually by knocking it.

Images and Solutions

Box (pictured)

The "default" state of the screen. The surroundings consist of an interface with 3 buttons and a variable monitor, as well as a guide on the wall to help with using the buttons. To solve the puzzle, type "HELLO" in Morse code using the "type" button (and the "clear" button, if need be). The "hear" button is used to listen to the message you must copy.

Light Bulb

The surroundings consist of 8 light switches in roughly a "sine wave" pattern. The solution is to (from left to right) turn the first, second, third, fifth, seventh, and eighth switches on. Leave the others off.

Hand

The surroundings consist of a stone, a sheet of paper, and a pair of scissors on and around the table, as well as writing on the wall saying "TRY TO LOSE". The spread-out hand is meant to represent a move of Paper in the game Rock Paper Scissors, so to lose, Rock must be thrown. Thus, the solution is to click on the stone.

Moon

The window takes on a different appearance. Otherwise, nothing is altered. The window in the Moon is altered when some of the puzzles are solved, now containing a question mark, a smiley face, and text partially obscured by the smiley face. In full, the text reads "bugs don't leave that easily, remember what feels useless". When all the puzzles are solved, the window is absent entirely, leaving in its place a dark gap of the same size. Clicking on the gap lets you leave the Dead Vermin Residence.

X-Ray

The surroundings only consist of a lowercase English letter, with an arrow pointing at the window. Knocking on the window changes the letter to the subsequent one in the alphabet. The solution is to knock on it until the letter is "x".

Puzzle Piece

The surroundings consist of a drawing of a light switch on the wall, alongside text reading "235 in Binary?". There is nothing to do here, it simply acts as a hint for the solution of Light Bulb.

Entryways

The only way to enter the Dead Vermin Residence is to press the Start button on the Title Screen.
